Frequently asked questions

Clarical shows no events or tasks

Open Settings ▸ Clarical and make sure Calendars and Reminders are set to Full Access.

My Google or Outlook calendar is missing

Clarical reads every calendar in Apple Calendar. Add your account in Settings ▸ Calendar ▸ Accounts ▸ Add Account, then switch on Calendars.

How does “Plan my day” work?

It places your tasks into the free gaps between your events, respecting your working hours, lunch and buffers. You see the plan first and choose Accept or Undo. It's free three times a week; Clarical Pro makes it unlimited.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Clarical ▸ Settings ▸ Clarical Pro ▸ Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with.

How do I cancel my subscription?

In the Settings app, tap your name ▸ Subscriptions ▸ Clarical Pro ▸ Cancel Subscription.

Travel time asks for my location. Why?

Only to work out when to leave for events that have a place. It's off unless you turn it on, and your location never leaves your device except to Apple Maps for the route.

Does Clarical work on Apple Watch?

Yes. Install it from the Watch app on your iPhone, then add the Up Next or Tasks Left complication to your watch face.
