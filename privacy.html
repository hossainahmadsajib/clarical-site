Clarical Privacy Policy

Effective: October 11, 2026 · Publisher: Dinero Automation LLC

Clarical is a day planner for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch. We built it so your schedule stays yours.

We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal data.

What Clarical accesses on your device

  • Calendars: to show your events and to create the time blocks you accept.
  • Reminders: to show, create, update and complete your tasks, using a “Clarical” list and any lists you choose.
  • Notifications: to send the morning plan, evening wrap-up and end-of-block alerts you turn on.
  • Location (optional): only if you turn on Travel time, to estimate when to leave for events with a place. The route is calculated by Apple Maps; we never receive your location.

This data is read and written through Apple's Calendar and Reminders on your device. If you use iCloud, Apple syncs it between your devices under Apple's Privacy Policy. Clarical has no servers of its own and never sends this data to us.

Text you type

When you type into “Add anything” or Brain dump, Clarical understands it on your device, using Apple's on-device intelligence where available or Clarical's built-in rules otherwise. The text is not sent to us or to any third party.

Settings and app data

Your preferences (working hours, routines, planning history) and task details such as estimated duration are stored only on your device, and in storage shared with Clarical's own widgets and extensions on that device.

Purchases

Clarical Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes payment; we receive no payment details and no personal information about you.

Analytics, ads and tracking

Clarical contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code, and does not track you across apps or websites.

Children

Clarical does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Your choices

You can turn Calendar, Reminders, location and notification access on or off at any time in Settings on your device. Deleting Clarical removes its on-device data; your calendar events and reminders stay in Apple's apps.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
